Facts about dogs

Dogs have a sense of time. It's been proven that they know the differenc e between an hour and five. If conditioned to, they can predict future events, such as regular walk times.

A study was conducted on dogs being away from their owners for varying hours and the studies show that dogs who were away from their owners the longest showed the most affection!
