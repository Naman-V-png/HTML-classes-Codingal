Facts about cats!

Cats are fascinating creatures that sleep 12-16 hours a day, possess 230 bones, and have unique nose prints similar to human fingerprints. They are obligate carnivores with exceptional night vision, 280-degree peripheral vision, and the ability to jump five times their height! Cats can even communicate with humans via meowing and use scent glands to mark territory!

Cats are fascinating creatures that share 95.6% of their DNA with tigers and can run up to 30 mph!
